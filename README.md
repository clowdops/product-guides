# ClowdOps documentation

**ClowdOps** is a platform for putting AI agents to work on the systems you already run. Each product is a purpose-built agent with its own tools, its own safety model, and its own guide — sharing one account, one organisation, and one balance.

## Products

| Product | What it does | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ClowdInfra** | Cloud infrastructure agent. Operate AWS, GCP, Azure and OCI from one conversation — provision, inspect, and remediate, with guardrails and budgets. | [Read the guide →](cloud-agent/README.md) |
| **ClowdBI** | Business-intelligence agent. Ask questions across your connected BI datasets and get governed, source-grounded answers and dashboards. | [Read the guide →](bi-agent/README.md) |

If your organisation is entitled to both, logging in lands you on the **product hub** where you pick one; the switcher at the top of the sidebar moves between them without logging out.
